nooon: Redesigning to Connect
Hotels and Influencers

Project Overview

nooon is a Helsinki-based startup that helps hotels collaborate with influencers to drive bookings. Their previous website lacked a cohesive visual identity,
and unclear messaging, which made it hard for users to understand the platform’s value. I led a full website redesign to improve clarity, usability, and engagement,
and implemented the English version of the site in WordPress, while collaborating with the CEO, sales, and development teams.

Context

Team Project | Sole UX Designer

My Role

UX/UI Design, Branding, Website Implementation

Timeline

3 weeks | June 2025

Tools

Figma, WordPress (Elementor
and Blocksy plugins), HTML/CSS

Why This Redesign Matters

  • The Challenge: The website was visually inconsistent, cluttered, and hard to navigate, leaving visitors confused about nooon’s value and services.
  • Design Objective: Redesign the website to reflect nooon’s professional B2B brand, improve clarity and usability, increase engagement, and support conversions.

Figure 1. Old nooon Website: Provides a snapshot of the site before redesign to establish a baseline for comparison.

Exploration & Discovery

I explored B2B SaaS and hospitality websites, including Notion, Monday.com, Mural, and Databox to gather ideas for layouts, typography, and features that reflected the CEO’s vision.

Figure 2. Website Inspirations: Showcases the visual styles and ideas the CEO wanted for nooon’s redesign.

I interviewed 5 stakeholders to understand current pain points, priorities, and the brand vision. Their input guided the site’s content strategy, key messaging, and functionality improvements.

From interviews with stakeholders and research on primary users (hoteliers of all sizes), I identified key needs for the website:

  • Clear explanation of nooon’s features and benefits
  • Easy-to-find calls-to-action (e.g., book a demo, sign up)
  • Organized content, including pricing, FAQs, and case studies

Based on interviews and research, I created three user personas

Figure 3. User Personas: Represents nooon’s primary users and their goals.

UX Design Process

Low-Fidelity Wireframes

I designed basic layouts to organize content, navigation, and hierarchy, focusing on clarity and simplicity. For the case studies page, I designed the main section with scalability in mind, so it can accommodate more than two case studies as the company grows.

Figure 4. Low-Fidelity Wireframes: Shows early layouts and page structures.

Mid-Fidelity Wireframes

I refined layouts, polished navigation, added copy, designed custom icons, and improved visual consistency.

Figure 5. Mid-Fidelity Wireframes: Shows improved navigation, copy, and icons.

Style Guide & Branding

I put together a style guide that tied everything together so that the brand felt consistent across the website and beyond.

Figure 6. Style Guide: Shows the logo, color palette, typography, and visual identity for nooon.

High-Fidelity Mockups

Using Figma, I finalized the typography, colors, spacing, and visuals to create a modern brand. I designed new sections like, “Book a Call,” pricing charts, and a reorganized FAQ, and a custom error page. On the Articles page, I used placeholder blocks to show the layout for dynamically AI-generated blog posts on the live site.

Since the company currently has only two case studies, I did not create a high-fidelity mockup of the Case Studies’ main page, but the lo and mid-fi wireframes were designed to scale as more case studies are added in the future.

Figure 6. High-Fidelity Mockups: Modern, minimalistic design reflecting the new brand identity.

Usability Testing & Iterations

I tested the live site with 4 team members from sales and marketing. Their feedback led me to small but meaningful updates, like adjusting button placement and refining call-to-action text to make the site easier to use.

Website Implementation

I implemented the English version of the site in WordPress using Elementor and Blocksy, customizing CSS and HTML for responsiveness and usability. You can view the live site here ↗

Impact

  • Improved clarity: Redesigning navigation, copy, and icons helped internal test participants quickly understand nooon’s platform and offerings.
  • Boosted engagement: Adding clear calls-to-action and reorganizing key content led to an increase in demo requests from stakeholders’ internal tracking.
  • Enhanced brand trust: Cohesive style guide, logo, and visuals made the website feel modern and professional; participants rated the site as visually consistent and credible.
